P2P privacy

Encrypted before protocol exchange begins.

On mainnet, testnet, and prunetest, nodes establish a hybrid encrypted session before exchanging Atho version, sync, block, or transaction messages.

Operators who want another layer of IP privacy can run a node on a VPS, or keep it on a local machine and route outbound traffic through an operator-managed VPN, TCP proxy, or tested Tor-compatible gateway. These are deployment choices outside Atho itself; direct peers see the connection's egress address.
