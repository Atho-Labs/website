Before you run

Keep the local boundary clear.

Use regnet

Keep disposable development state away from mainnet and retired testnet data.

Encrypt wallets

Use a strong password and keep the recovery phrase offline.

Keep RPC local

Raw RPC and HTTP must remain on loopback or behind an encrypted operator boundary.

Verify releases

Match source revisions, checksums, signatures, and upgrade notes before production use.
